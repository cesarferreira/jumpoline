# Jumpoline!

[Play Jumpoline on GitHub Pages](https://cesarferreira.com/jumpoline/)

Bounce up trampolines, dodge flying bananas, and collect stars on your way through 20 levels to the moon. Every four stars charge a boost, and stars collected across runs unlock new animals.

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Left and right arrows or A and D | Hold a direction button or either side of the playfield |
| Boost | Space or Up arrow | Tap Boost |
| Pause | Esc | Tap Pause |

Relaxed mode gives you unlimited rescues. Challenge mode gives you three hearts. Your best height, total stars, and settings are saved in your browser.

To play locally, open `index.html` in a browser. The game has no dependencies or build step.
